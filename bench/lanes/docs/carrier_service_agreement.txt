Carrier Service Agreement - Kestrel Freight Sdn Bhd

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark. Kestrel Freight is
an invented carrier.

1. Service. Kestrel Freight carries outbound and inter-warehouse loads across
Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah and Sarawak.

2. Delivery standard. Peninsular deliveries are made within 2 business days and
deliveries to Sabah and Sarawak within 5 business days.

3. On-time target. Kestrel Freight commits to an on-time delivery rate of 96%
each month.

4. Service credit. If the monthly on-time rate falls below target, Kestrel
Freight issues a service credit of 5% of that month's freight charges.

5. Liability. Liability for loss or damage is capped at RM 20 per kilogram.

6. Claims. Damage claims must be lodged within 7 days of delivery.

7. Fuel surcharge. The fuel surcharge is reviewed every quarter.
