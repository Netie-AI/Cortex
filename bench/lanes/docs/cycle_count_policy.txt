Inventory Cycle Count Policy

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark.

1. A-class SKUs are counted every month, B-class SKUs every quarter and C-class
SKUs twice a year.

2. Variance tolerance. A count variance within 2% of the system quantity is
accepted without a recount.

3. Recount. A variance above 2% triggers a blind recount by a second counter
within 48 hours.

4. Write-off approval. Inventory adjustments above RM 10,000 need the
approval of the Finance Controller.

5. Freeze. Stock movements in a bin are frozen for the duration of its count.

6. Year-end. A full wall-to-wall count takes place in the first week of
December.
