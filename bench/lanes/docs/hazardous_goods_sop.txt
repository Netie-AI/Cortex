Standard Operating Procedure - Hazardous Goods Storage

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark.

1. Applies to. Every inventory item marked hazardous, including CHEMICALS and
CLEANING stock.

2. Segregation. Oxidisers are stored at least 5 metres away from flammable
liquids.

3. Stacking. Hazardous drums are stacked no more than 2 pallets high.

4. Quantity limit. No single bin may hold more than 500 kg of flammable liquid.

5. Protective equipment. Staff handling corrosives wear chemical goggles,
nitrile gloves and an apron.

6. Spills. A spill is contained within 10 minutes and reported to the Safety
Officer on the same shift.

7. Documents. A safety data sheet must be kept at the bin for every hazardous
SKU.

8. Training. Hazardous goods handlers repeat their training every 12 months.

9. Approved site. Bulk chemicals are stored only at WH-P Kemaman Chemical Park.
