Warehouse Safety Policy

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark.

1. Forklifts. Forklifts travel at no more than 8 km/h inside the warehouse and
3 km/h in pedestrian zones.

2. Licences. Only staff holding a valid forklift licence may operate a forklift,
and licences are refreshed every 2 years.

3. Racking. Racking is inspected every 6 months by a qualified inspector.

4. Incidents. Every incident or near miss is reported within 24 hours.

5. Fire drills. A fire evacuation drill is held every 3 months.

6. Footwear. Safety shoes are compulsory on the warehouse floor.

7. Working at height. Work above 2 metres needs a harness and a permit.
