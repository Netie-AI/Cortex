Standard Operating Procedure - Cold Chain Handling

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark.

1. Applies to. FOOD_COLD and MEDICAL stock held at the cold-storage sites.

2. Temperature range. Chilled stock is held between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius.
Frozen stock is held at minus 18 degrees Celsius or colder.

3. Monitoring. Cold room temperature is logged every 15 minutes by the data
logger and checked by a supervisor twice per shift.

4. Excursions. A temperature excursion lasting more than 30 minutes must be
reported to the Quality Lead within 1 hour, and the affected stock is placed on
quality hold.

5. Dock time. Chilled pallets may stay on the receiving dock for no more than
20 minutes before they enter the cold room.

6. Door discipline. Cold room doors must not be left open for longer than
90 seconds.

7. Cold room capacity. The WH-B cold room is rated for 9,500 kg of chilled
stock.

8. Calibration. Temperature probes are calibrated every 6 months.
