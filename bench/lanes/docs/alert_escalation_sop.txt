Standard Operating Procedure - Alert Escalation

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark.

1. Acknowledgement. A CRITICAL alert is acknowledged within 15 minutes, a HIGH
alert within 1 hour, a MEDIUM alert within 4 hours and a LOW alert by the next
business day.

2. Escalation. An unresolved CRITICAL alert is escalated to the Regional
Operations Manager after 2 hours.

3. Supplier risk alerts. A SUPPLIER_RISK alert opens a review with Procurement
within 3 business days.

4. Capacity warnings. A CAPACITY_WARNING is raised when a site passes 90% of
its rated capacity.

5. Closure. An alert is closed only after the root cause is recorded.

6. Reporting. A weekly alert summary goes to the site managers every Monday.
