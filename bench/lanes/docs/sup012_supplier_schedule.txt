Supplier Schedule - Malaysia Supplier 012 Sdn Bhd (SUP-012)

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark. Figures differ from
the suppliers table on purpose.

Schedule to the Master Supply Agreement for SUP-012.

A. Lead time. SUP-012 delivers within 12 calendar days of purchase order
acceptance.

B. Payment terms. Invoices from SUP-012 are payable Net 30 from receipt of a
complete delivery.

C. Contracted SKUs. SUP-012 is the contracted source for SKU-00307 and SKU-00412.

D. Delivery location. SUP-012 delivers only to WH-B Penang Distribution.

E. Volume rebate. SUP-012 grants a 3% rebate once annual purchases exceed
RM 250,000.

F. Quality. A delivery with more than 2% damaged units may be rejected in full.

G. Review. This schedule is reviewed every 6 months.
