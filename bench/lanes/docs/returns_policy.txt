Customer Returns Policy

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark.

1. Return window. Customers may return unopened goods within 30 days of
delivery.

2. Restocking fee. A restocking fee of 15% applies to returns of non-defective
goods.

3. Defective goods. Defective goods are replaced or refunded in full, with no
restocking fee, if reported within 90 days.

4. Excluded items. FOOD_COLD and MEDICAL items cannot be returned once they
have left the cold chain.

5. Authorisation. Every return needs a return merchandise authorisation number
issued by Customer Service.

6. Refund timing. Refunds are paid within 14 days of the returned goods being
inspected.
