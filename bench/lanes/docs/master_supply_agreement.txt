Master Supply Agreement - Warehouse Network Suppliers

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark. Every name and figure
here is invented and deliberately differs from the warehouse tables.

1. Scope. This agreement governs every supplier that delivers stock into the
warehouse network. A Supplier Schedule may vary a clause for one supplier; where
it does not, this agreement applies.

2. Standard lead time. The standard lead time is 14 calendar days from purchase
order acceptance to delivery at the receiving dock.

3. Payment terms. Invoices are payable Net 45 from the date a complete delivery is
received, not from the invoice date.

4. Late delivery penalty. For each day a delivery is late the supplier pays a
penalty of 0.5% of the order value, capped at 10% of the order value.

5. Freight. Freight is paid by the supplier for orders above RM 5,000. Below that
value freight is paid by the buyer.

6. Minimum order. The minimum order value is RM 1,200 per purchase order.

7. Price changes. The supplier must give 60 days written notice of any price
increase.

8. Warranty. Goods carry a warranty of 12 months from the date of delivery.

9. Termination. Either party may terminate this agreement with 90 days written
notice.

10. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of Malaysia and disputes
go to arbitration in Kuala Lumpur.
