Procurement Approval Policy

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark.

1. Approval limits. Purchase orders up to RM 20,000 are approved by the
Procurement Manager. Orders above RM 20,000 and up to RM 100,000 need the Head
of Operations. Orders above RM 100,000 need the Chief Financial Officer.

2. Quotes. Purchases above RM 50,000 need three written quotes.

3. Supplier audits. Every active supplier is audited at least once every
18 months.

4. High-risk suppliers. A supplier with a risk score above 0.6 needs a
quarterly business review.

5. New suppliers. A new supplier is onboarded only after a credit check and a
site visit.

6. Single sourcing. A single-source purchase needs a written justification
signed by the Head of Operations.
