Supplier Schedule - China Supplier 109 Sdn Bhd (SUP-109)

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark. Figures differ from
the suppliers table on purpose.

Schedule to the Master Supply Agreement for SUP-109.

A. Lead time. SUP-109 ships by sea; the agreed lead time is 45 calendar days
from purchase order acceptance.

B. Incoterms. Deliveries are made on CIF Port Klang terms.

C. Payment terms. 30% of the order value is paid as a deposit and the balance
is payable Net 30 after arrival at Port Klang.

D. Currency. SUP-109 invoices in US dollars.

E. Inspection. A pre-shipment inspection by an independent inspector is
required for every container.

F. Contracted category. SUP-109 supplies MACHINERY_PARTS, mainly bearings and
hydraulic seals.
