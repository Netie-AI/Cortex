Expiry and Disposal Policy

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark.

1. Picking rule. Stock is picked first-expiry-first-out.

2. Near-expiry flag. An item is flagged as near expiry 60 days before its
expiry date.

3. Markdown. Near-expiry dry goods may be sold at a markdown of up to 40%.

4. Disposal. Expired stock is disposed of within 7 days of its expiry date.

5. Approval. Disposal of expired stock worth more than RM 5,000 needs approval
from the Operations Manager.

6. Scheduled waste. Expired chemicals are handed to a licensed scheduled-waste
contractor, never put in general waste.

7. Records. Disposal certificates are kept for 7 years.
