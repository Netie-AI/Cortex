Standard Operating Procedure - Inbound Receiving

Fictional measurement document for the LANES-V1 benchmark.

1. Booking. Suppliers book a dock slot at least 24 hours before arrival.

2. Unloading. A booked truck is unloaded within 45 minutes of docking.

3. Inspection. Every delivery is inspected and counted within 4 hours of
unloading.

4. Discrepancy tolerance. A quantity difference of up to 1% against the
delivery note is accepted; a larger difference is raised as a discrepancy.

5. Quarantine. Damaged or unlabelled goods go to the quarantine bay for a
maximum of 5 working days while the supplier responds.

6. Put-away. Accepted stock is put away within 8 hours of inspection.

7. Records. The goods received note is posted on the same day as receipt.
